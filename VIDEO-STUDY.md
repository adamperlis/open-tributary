## Method and limits

The watch skill downloaded 22 public embedded MP4 clips from the case-study page. Selected frames from every clip were inspected in chronological order. Sampling was capped at three frames per clip, with ten frames for the five-second flow loop. Perceptual deduplication removed held frames. This supports visual structure and broad motion direction, not frame-accurate timing analysis. No transcript was available and Whisper was disabled; no audio claims are made. The separate Vimeo film in Codrops returned HTTP 401 and was not watched.

Installed ffmpeg rejected the watch script’s retired `-vsync` flag. A temporary copy of the scripts used its current equivalent, `-fps_mode vfr`; installed skill files were unchanged. Analysis reports, downloaded sources, and frame images remain under `/private/tmp/tributary-windsurf-study` for follow-up. They are not bundled into this website.

## Translation into Tributary

Use a confluence rather than a wave as the central form. Keep the creator as the royalty recipient. Three original gradient ribbons identify sample products and return toward the creator. Text, nodes, and reversible arrow markers explain the two directions. Ribbon widths are illustrative, with no implied payment amounts.

The painting supplies warm parchment, olive, gold, vermilion, and blue. Cream carries the proposition and documents. A permanent forest field gives the homepage diagram and community section contrast. Fine contour lines lend a drawn quality without introducing a texture that competes with text.

The identity study contains two original options: a compact confluence “t” and a larger flowing wordmark. The complete redesign now uses the compact confluence mark in navigation. No reference logo, proprietary font, video, painting, or photograph is used as a site asset.

Motion is tied to selecting a flow mode: arrows reverse and explanatory text changes. Transitions finish within 240ms. Reduced motion removes transitions. Brand-loop animation is left out of long-form reading surfaces.
